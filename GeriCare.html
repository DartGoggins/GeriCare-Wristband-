The study proposes GeriCare, a standalone 4G LTE–enabled smart wristband designed for the periodic monitoring of vital signs in geriatric patients under the care of licensed caregivers in Home for the Aged institutions in Cebu City.

It seeks to understand the experiences and challenges faced by caregivers in manually monitoring the vital signs of geriatric patients — particularly in relation to caregiver workload, missed or delayed monitoring, patient safety, and the need for automated monitoring.

We invite qualified licensed caregivers who have experience caring for geriatric patients and monitoring their vital signs to participate. Honest and unbiased responses will help the researchers assess the potential usefulness and feasibility of the proposed GeriCare Smart Wristband.

Participation is voluntary. You may choose not to participate or withdraw at any time. In accordance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), all information and responses are treated with confidentiality and used solely for academic and research purposes.
§ Capabilities

What the wristband reads.

Four vital signs and one panic input — measured on a schedule the caregiver sets, transmitted over cellular the moment they are captured.

Heart rate

Continuous PPG sampling via the MAX30102 optical sensor pressed against the underside of the wrist.

01 / bpm

Blood oxygen

SpO₂ derived from red / infrared light absorption — the same principle as clinical fingertip pulse oximeters.

02 / %

Body temperature

Non-contact infrared reads skin temperature at set intervals — no disruption to sleep or rest.

03 / °C

4G LTE uplink

Every reading is transmitted over cellular directly — the wristband works without Wi-Fi or a paired phone.

04 / net
§ Target Users

Two hands, one wrist.

GeriCare is designed for two people at once — the licensed caregiver who monitors, and the geriatric patient who is monitored.

Primary Operator

The licensed caregiver.

Caregivers in Home for the Aged institutions often monitor many residents at once. GeriCare reduces manual rounds, minimizes missed readings, and surfaces alerts the moment a vital falls out of range.
